## 1. Executive summary

**Verdict: NOT READY — ready with fixes after one revision pass.**

The specs are well structured, traceable and far above V1 in rigour. But they are not yet "no gaps, no failures": there are six issues that would produce a concrete failure or a wrong build on the P1 critical path (Meta messaging window, Meta permissions, contradictory scope statement, stock-mode semantics, AI tool idempotency on retries, a money-rule contradiction), and a long tail of ambiguities in plan limits, migration mapping, notification coverage and delivery rules. Nothing found requires re-architecting; everything is fixable in the spec text.

| Severity | Count |
|---|---|
| Blocker | 6 |
| Major | 36 |
| Minor | 48 |
| **Total** | **90** |

Category split: A (V1 coverage/factual) 19 · B (CDC / invoice) 10 · C (scope creep) 7 · D (internal consistency) 19 · E (completeness) 31 · F (technical risk) 4.

What is solid (no finding): invoice items 01–07 are all mapped to requirement IDs; CDC UC01–UC06 nominal paths are covered; the 7 analytics tabs, 23 reports, GDS modules, order call-confirmation flow, caisse "cash actually moved" rule, i18n FR/AR/EN+RTL, and the Figma component list (08 §1) match V1/Figma exactly; the V1 security gaps listed in 01 §1 / 09 §1 are all factually confirmed in code (unsigned Chargily webhook `server.ts:209-231`, unsigned Meta webhook `webhook.controller.ts:157-198`, plaintext page tokens `pages.controller.ts:191,204`, 30-day JWT `auth.controller.ts:45`, in-memory batcher `message-batcher.ts:43-44`, Expo push with no client `push.service.ts:9`, no plan-limit enforcement `user-agents.controller.ts:10`).
